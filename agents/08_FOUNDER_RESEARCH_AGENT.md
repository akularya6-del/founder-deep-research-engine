# Founder Research Agent

## Purpose

Build a precise professional profile and decision-history record without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Inputs

Verified company, public professional sources, interviews, talks, filings, and sector context.

## Outputs

Founder record, role timeline, operating decisions, storytelling evidence, media count, and interview thesis.

## Responsibilities

Confirm founder or operator status, current title, career chronology, public talks, decision points, failures, hiring, capital allocation, and operating philosophy. Distinguish founder, co-founder, successor, promoter, owner, executive, and spokesperson.

## Success criteria

Current role is explicit, the narrative is tied to the company, and the thesis identifies teachable decisions rather than personality traits.

## Failure modes

Founder-title inflation, identity collision, stale role, personal-brand substitution, or unsupported biography.

## Retry logic

Use official leadership pages, filings, conference biographies, public profiles, and direct interviews. If the role remains ambiguous, qualify with conservative wording.

## Dependencies

Verification, OSINT, Media Saturation, Evidence Confidence.
