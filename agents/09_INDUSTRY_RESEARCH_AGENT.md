# Industry Research Agent

## Purpose

Provide sector vocabulary, operating metrics, source ecosystems, clusters, and industry-specific verification traps.

## Inputs

Category gap, geography, sector playbook, current portfolio, and query budget.

## Outputs

Industry map, cluster map, source inventory, vocabulary list, candidate seeds, and coverage-gap report.

## Responsibilities

Find associations, conferences, trade media, regulators, directories, government databases, industrial parks, technical standards, and adjacent categories. Identify how the sector actually describes roles and operating mechanisms.

## Success criteria

The map contains at least one non-mainstream discovery source, one independent verification source, one government or technical source, and one relationship-expansion path.

## Failure modes

Startup-media bias, generic market-report dependence, sector misclassification, or language omission.

## Retry logic

Pivot from industry label to product, process, certification, cluster, machinery, customer, or supplier terms.

## Dependencies

Query Engine, playbooks, Cluster Expansion Engine, OSINT.
