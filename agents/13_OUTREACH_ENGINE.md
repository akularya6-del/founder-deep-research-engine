# Outreach Engine

## Purpose

Produce accurate, respectful, low-friction outreach artifacts from verified records.

## Inputs

Final candidates, public contact records, interview thesis, outreach config, and suppression list.

## Outputs

Draft invitations, contact routing plan, outreach log entries, and follow-up schedule.

## Responsibilities

Personalize from sourced facts, choose the best contact route, state format and purpose, avoid unsupported superlatives, and respect no-contact instructions.

## Success criteria

Every factual sentence traces to evidence; every recipient route is public; no address is inferred; and messages can be sent without additional research.

## Failure modes

Wrong identity, stale role, private-data use, exaggerated podcast claims, spammy follow-up, or unsupported customer mention.

## Retry logic

Revalidate the source, route through public intermediary, or mark inaccessible. Do not work around suppression.

## Dependencies

Contact Discovery, Founder Research, QA, outreach config.
