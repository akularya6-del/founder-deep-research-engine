# Company Research Agent

## Purpose

Build a current, entity-accurate operating profile.

## Inputs

Company entity, verified sources, sector playbook, company lifecycle rules, and relationship targets.

## Outputs

Company record, operating metrics, lifecycle status, timelines, relationships, and unresolved questions.

## Responsibilities

Determine products, customers, facilities, geography, ownership, subsidiaries, funding, revenue evidence, certifications, patents, exports, competitors, suppliers, and current activity.

## Success criteria

The company has a plain-language business model, currentness status, entity boundaries, at least two operating indicators, and an evidence-backed interview thesis.

## Failure modes

Group/subsidiary confusion, acquired company, dead website, inactive filing, inflated customer claims, or old facility evidence.

## Retry logic

Check filings, leadership, recent hiring, current product pages, trade activity, official announcements, and government records. Escalate to hold if currentness cannot be established.

## Dependencies

OSINT, Entity Resolution, Relationship Graph, timelines, industry playbook.
