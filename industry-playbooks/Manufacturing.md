# Manufacturing Industry Playbook

## Purpose

Provide sector-specific discovery, verification, scoring, and interview guidance for the autonomous research system.

## Vocabulary

Use the sector’s own operating language before startup language. Expand terms for products, processes, facilities, quality, procurement, customers, certifications, regulation, and distribution. Add local-language equivalents and role titles.

## Key metrics

Track the metrics that reveal operating substance: capacity, utilization, throughput, yield, defects, lead time, working capital, gross margin, retention, implementation time, customer concentration, approval cycle, delivery reliability, and capital intensity. Use sector-specific metrics only when definitions are clear.

## Founder profiles

Prioritize founders, owners, promoters, managing directors, technical operators, second-generation leaders, and executives with current operating responsibility. A speaker or award recipient is a lead, not proof.

## High-value sources

Search official company pages, regulators, filings, associations, trade fairs, industrial parks, technical journals, government databases, customer announcements, patents, conference programs, trade directories, and local-language business media.

## Discovery patterns

Search the sector with founder, owner, managing director, plant, capacity, export, certification, tender, supplier, customer, cluster, conference, award, patent, filing, and industrial park terms. Pivot from company to founder and from founder to plant, products, customers, suppliers, competitors, and contacts.

## Interview angles

Ask about the initial constraint, the most important operating decision, the tradeoff that outsiders miss, how quality is measured, how pricing is set, how working capital is managed, how people and process interact, what failed, and what the current system looks like.

## Hallucination traps

Do not convert marketing language into capacity, “global” into named countries, customer logos into confirmed customers, a patent into commercial success, an award into leadership, an AI label into proprietary technology, a project announcement into completion, or a directory listing into current operations.

## Verification strategy

Confirm exact entity, current role, product, facility, date, ownership, lifecycle status, and at least two independent operating signals. Use the source-family detector, entity resolver, and confidence engine. Preserve conflicting values rather than averaging incompatible definitions.

## Output

Return an industry map, candidates, source families, high-signal queries, verification questions, relationship seeds, and a gap report.
